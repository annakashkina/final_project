Privacy

codeprobe — university final project

By default, nothing is stored

When you use codeprobe without enabling "save my progress", your learning is completely private. No data is stored on the server or in your browser. Conversations with the AI tutor are processed in real time and not recorded.

When you enable saving

If you toggle "save my progress" (which you can revoke at any time), we store:

  • A random identifier (not your name, email, or any personal info)
  • Which lessons you completed and when
  • Your conversations with the AI tutor (questions and answers)
  • Your learning choices (mode, language, questions asked)
  • A timestamp of when you gave consent

Saving is opt-in and based on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by toggling saving off.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, or any account information
  • Your IP address or geographic location
  • Your browser or device information
  • Cookies or third-party trackers

AI provider

Conversations are sent to Groq (United States) for AI tutoring. These requests are anonymous — they contain only the lesson code and your messages, with no identifying information. Groq operates under EU Standard Contractual Clauses for cross-border data transfers. Zero data retention is enabled — Groq does not store inputs or outputs.

Security and rate limiting

To protect the service from abuse, your IP address is processed in server memory for rate limiting. IP addresses are never written to disk, logged, or shared — they are held in memory only and cleared when the server restarts.

Data retention

If you enable saving, your data is kept for up to 90 days after your last activity. After that, it is automatically deleted. You can also delete your data at any time using the controls on the home page.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on a server in the European Economic Area (Netherlands). It is not shared with any third parties beyond the AI provider described above.

Your rights

  • Pause — toggle saving off at any time; recording stops immediately
  • Resume — toggle saving back on without losing your existing record
  • Export — download all your stored data as a JSON file
  • Delete — permanently remove all your data from the server

All controls are on the home page. No account or login needed. If you believe your data rights have not been respected, you can contact your local data protection authority.
