Privacy

codeprobe

By default, nothing is stored

When you use codeprobe without enabling "save my progress", your learning is completely private. No data is stored on the server or in your browser. Conversations with the AI tutor are processed in real time and not recorded.

When you enable saving

If you toggle "save my progress", we store:

  • A random identifier (not your name, email, or any personal info)
  • Which lessons you completed and when
  • Your conversations with the AI tutor (questions and answers)
  • Your learning choices (mode, language, questions asked)
  • A timestamp of when you gave consent

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, or any account information
  • Your IP address or geographic location
  • Your browser or device information
  • Cookies or third-party trackers

AI provider

Conversations are sent to the configured AI provider for tutoring. These requests are anonymous — they contain only the lesson code and your messages, with no other information that identifies you. The AI provider's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Your rights

  • Pause — toggle saving off at any time; recording stops immediately
  • Resume — toggle saving back on without losing your existing record
  • Export — download all your stored data as a JSON file
  • Delete — permanently remove all your data from the server

All controls are available on the home page. No account or login is needed.

Data storage

Data is stored as files on the server hosting this application. It is not shared with any third parties beyond the AI provider used for tutoring.
